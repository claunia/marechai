MS Sans Serif and MS Sans Serif Bold — pixel-accurate FontStruct
reconstructions of the Microsoft Sans Serif raster font that shipped with
Windows 95, by "lou".

Sources:
  https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384746  (Regular)
  https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384862  (Bold)
