CDE (Solaris) theme — bundled font
===================================

Files: luxi-sans.woff2, luxi-sans-bold.woff2
  (format-only conversion of luxisr.ttf and luxisb.ttf from X.Org
   font-bh-ttf-1.0.3 via woff2_compress; no subsetting, no glyph
   modification — only container repackaging from TTF to WOFF2.)
